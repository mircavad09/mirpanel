# Delivered product sales · 2026-11-14

## Cause

The live snapshot selected payment status `approved/completed`, rather than the delivery ledger. Wallet orders can remain `reviewing` while their customer-facing delivery status is completed. All 116 wallet-delivered units were missing; 11 card-delivered units were already represented by the legacy status query. One additional legacy single-product quantity was also previously counted as one order instead of two units.

## Counting and compatibility

New sales use persisted `customer_order_deliveries` quantities, grouped by order and line, bounded by purchased quantity. Repeated reads/status changes/restarts create no increments. Wallet, ready-stock, supplier and manual deliveries share this ledger. Rejected/expired and unpaid orders do not count. Cancellation retains only actually delivered units. Manual customer links live in `netflix_room_legacy` and are not sales.

The migration freezes only previously admitted untracked legacy approvals into a private compatibility table, once. A delivery-tracked order replaces its fallback rather than adding to it. New approvals without delivery never enter that fallback. No baseline, order, delivery, customer balance or credential is updated. The public snapshot exposes aggregated counts only. Re-running both this migration and the older cart installer preserves the rule.

All three customer views re-read the same no-store endpoint every 10 seconds while visible and on return/focus. Home cache expires after 5 seconds. Asset versions are advanced for existing sessions on reload.
