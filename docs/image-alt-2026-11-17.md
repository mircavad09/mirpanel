# Image alternatives — 17 November 2026

## Baseline

Live home HTML contained 21 images; the hydrated page contained 38 at both 390px and 1440px. No image lacked the `alt` attribute in these current snapshots. Bing's reported 12 cases relate to its inspected/indexed snapshot; their exact element mapping was not available through the disconnected browser and is not assumed.

The quick-links strip has 12 decorative logos (six repeated for scrolling). Each already has `alt=""`, an `aria-hidden` wrapper and adjacent visible product name; the repeated group is also hidden from accessibility. These alternatives must not be filled with repeated SEO keywords just to suppress a checker warning. Story thumbnails also accompany a visible title and labelled button, so their empty alternatives remain intentional.

## Changes

- Shared catalog cards trim custom alternatives and fall back to the product name if the configured value is whitespace.
- Legacy catalog/similar-product images now receive escaped product descriptions instead of empty alternatives.
- Product detail and purchase-dialog image alternatives are updated when the product changes, avoiding a generic English placeholder or stale previous-product name.
- The unused home product image placeholder stays empty until it has a product/source.
- Four managed product banners use their linked product and package destination rather than the generic CMS title. The support banner describes operator contact. Meaningful custom descriptions remain intact; unlinked decorative banners stay empty.
- Changed script versions prevent cached old components from taking precedence. Product HTML was regenerated with the new script versions. Prices, stock and ordering rules are unchanged.

## Verification

`test-image-alt.mjs`: initial home and all 13 generated product HTML documents have explicit alternatives; escaping, whitespace fallback, older cached card compatibility, changing-product alternatives and banner descriptions tested. Chrome and WebKit at 390/1440px passed. `test-seo.mjs` passed. The full local SEO/purchase-selection browser regression passed 78/78 synthetic cases (Chrome 320/390/430/1440; WebKit 390/1440), with no real purchase.

Live audit artifacts are under `outputs/seo/alt-before.json` and `alt-after.json` (the latter is created after deployment). A live browser audit is not Bing's Live URL test. Bing Live URL and Request indexing must only be reported as completed after their own confirmation is observed.
